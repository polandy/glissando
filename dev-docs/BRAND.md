# Brand

The name: _glissando_ (Italian, "gliding") — sliding from one note to the next; here, fluid
transitions from one picture to the next. Character: a calm, professional tool — neutral surfaces, the pictures bring the colour; the
logo keeps the playful touch. The words the app uses, one per concept in English and German:
[COPY.md](COPY.md).

## Logo

Three fanned, slightly rotated picture cards (peach, mint, lemon) with an outline; the front card
shows a sun and a mountain. Below it the wordmark "Glissando", rotated −3°.

| File (`assets/brand/`)      | Use                                                                        |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `logo-mark.svg`             | Mark alone, light background                                               |
| `logo-stacked-light.svg`    | Mark + wordmark, light background                                          |
| `logo-stacked-dark.svg`     | Mark + wordmark, transparent, for `#171223`                                |
| `logo-stacked-animated.svg` | Mark + wordmark in design tokens, with class hooks for the start animation |
| `app-icon.svg`              | App icon, 512 × 512                                                        |
| `app-icon-maskable.svg`     | App icon full bleed, logo inside the safe zone (Android, iOS home screen)  |
| `favicon.svg`               | Two cards only, for 16–32 px                                               |

The wordmark is outlined to paths, so the logos render without the font. The stacked logos and
the webfonts are generated by `scripts/brand/build.sh`; edit the script, not the outputs. It also
renders the PNG app icons in `public/icons/` (`scripts/brand/icons.sh`).

### Start animation

On the app's very first launch the logo assembles itself ("glide", about 1.4 s): the three cards
glide in from the left one after another, the sun and mountain pop up on the front card, then the
wordmark is revealed from left to right. A tap anywhere jumps to the finished logo. With reduced
motion set in the system, every part only fades in (0.4 s), without movement. Every later launch
shows the finished logo at once; the launch is remembered in the browser's local storage.

## Type

All OFL 1.1, self-hosted as Latin woff2 subsets incl. German (`assets/fonts/`, licence texts
beside them), fetched from a pinned commit of google/fonts and hash-verified by
`scripts/brand/build.sh`; no request leaves the device. Each shipped font's OFL notice is also
listed in `third-party-licenses.md` (`build/committed-licenses.ts`), since none is an npm
package.

| Font (token)                                   | Role                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Instrument Sans 400/500/600 (`--gl-font-ui`)   | Everything you operate and read: labels, buttons, body   |
| Bricolage Grotesque 700 (`--gl-font-display`)  | Titles only: screen headings, slideshow names            |
| Geist Mono 400/500 (`--gl-font-mono`, `.mono`) | Times, counts, dates — with tabular figures so they hold |
| Baloo 2 800 (`--gl-font-wordmark`)             | The "Glissando" wordmark in the header and the logo      |
| Instrument Serif 400, italic (card only)       | The "Classic" title card style (ADR-0032), nowhere else  |

Baloo 2 is for the brand alone: the header wordmark and the logo / start animation (whose
wordmark is outlined to paths). Sizes come from the `--gl-size-*` scale: caption 11, small 12,
meta 12.5, label 13, body 14, large button 15, title 16, name 17, wordmark 19, panel title 22,
heading 26 (23 below 720 px), display 34 px.

Title cards (ADR-0032) are drawn by the player and take their colours from the **card
palette** in `card-palette.ts`, not from the tokens: `plum` `#2A2340`, `night` `#14111C`, `cream`
`#FFF3E2`, `peach` `#FFB59A`, `mint` `#8FD9C4`, `lemon` `#FFD95A`; light ink `#FFFFFF`, dark ink
`#2A2340`.

Rules:

- Status colours are fills, borders and tints (mixed into the surface), never body text.
- The logo keeps the Sorbet palette: peach `#FFB59A`, mint `#8FD9C4`, lemon `#FFD95A`, outline
  plum `#2A2340`; in dark mode the cards are outlined in `#171223` (sticker look).
- The theme follows `prefers-color-scheme`; `data-theme="light|dark"` on `<html>` pins one (see
  [app/THEMES.md](app/THEMES.md)).

Shape: `--gl-radius` 10 px (buttons, notices, summary cells), `--gl-radius-large` 14 px (cards,
panels, dialogs), `--gl-radius-tile` 8 px (picture tiles), `--gl-radius-tiny` 3 px (the
prepare screen's picture squares), hairlines instead of shadows.
Touch: under a coarse pointer `.btn`, `.icon-btn` and the switch keep their look and grow an invisible
pad to `--gl-hit-min` (44 px); neighbours need room for both pads, or a gap of at least the overhang.
Layer: `--gl-layer-bar` (5) puts a sticky bottom bar above the content that scrolls under it; a tile
isolates its own marks, so their z-index stays local.

## Icons

Line icons on a 24-unit grid, stroke 1.7 with round caps and joins (play and pause filled), listed
once in `src/ui-kit/icons.ts` and drawn by `src/app/components/Icon.svelte`. An icon next to a
label is hidden from screen readers; an icon-only button carries an `aria-label`.
